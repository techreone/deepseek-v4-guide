### What Is DeepSeek AI?

DeepSeek is a Chinese artificial intelligence company founded in 2023. It develops large language models and AI systems for reasoning, coding, text generation, document work, research, and application development. Users can interact with DeepSeek through the official chat and app, while developers can connect products and workflows through the API or evaluate eligible open-weight releases in their own infrastructure.

The name can be confusing because it describes several related things. DeepSeek Chat is the hosted user interface. DeepSeek Platform is the developer account and billing area. The API is the programmable service used by apps and automations. Model names such as V4, R1, V3, Coder, Math, VL, and OCR refer to different releases or research families. These access paths should not be treated as identical products with identical features, privacy terms, or infrastructure requirements.


#### What can people use DeepSeek for?

- Writing and communication: outlines, drafts, rewrites, translations, summaries, and structured notes.

- Learning and explanation: breaking down concepts, comparing ideas, creating study questions, and exploring examples.

- Coding: explaining code, drafting functions, debugging, generating tests, documenting systems, and planning migrations.

- Document work: extracting themes, comparing passages, organizing information, and drafting source-grounded summaries when the application supplies reliable context.

- Developer applications: chatbots, internal assistants, classification, structured output, tool-using workflows, and controlled automation.

- RAG and knowledge systems: generating answers from approved documents when retrieval, permissions, citations, and evaluation are implemented correctly.

- Open-weight evaluation: testing eligible releases locally or in private infrastructure, subject to model licenses and operational requirements.

Important limitation: DeepSeek can produce incorrect, incomplete, outdated, biased, or fabricated information. Verify consequential outputs against reliable sources and require qualified human review for legal, medical, financial, security, employment, education, and other high-impact decisions.

### Privacy, Security, and Safer Use

DeepSeek can be useful for everyday and business tasks, but the right safety decision depends on the data, access path, jurisdiction, and consequences of an incorrect answer. Official chat, the official API, Chat-Deep.ai, a third-party integration, and a self-hosted model are different environments. Review each environment separately.


#### Do not paste secrets

Keep passwords, API keys, private signing keys, authentication tokens, confidential contracts, customer records, health data, financial records, and sensitive personal information out of unapproved AI tools.


#### Verify important answers

Check claims against primary sources. Test code before deployment. Require a qualified reviewer before using AI output for legal, medical, financial, security, employment, compliance, or customer-facing decisions.


#### Control production access

Store API keys server-side, limit tool permissions, separate users and tenants, log safely, monitor spend, filter sensitive data, defend against prompt injection, and require approval for consequential actions.


#### Does self-hosting make DeepSeek private?

Not automatically. Downloading eligible model weights can give a team more infrastructure control, but privacy still depends on the complete environment: hosting, network rules, telemetry, plugins, logs, storage, backups, access controls, administrators, patches, and connected tools. Treat a local deployment as private only after reviewing the full system.


#### Regional and workplace restrictions

Access rules and organizational policies can differ by country, government, employer, school, and regulated sector. A restriction on government devices does not automatically describe consumer access, API access, or every independent self-hosted deployment. Check authoritative local guidance and your organization’s policy instead of relying on a global yes-or-no statement.
