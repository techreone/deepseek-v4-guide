Chat-Deep.ai is an independent editorial and technical website that provides a browser-based AI chat interface and practical information about DeepSeek models, APIs, pricing, access, safety, and related workflows. Our purpose is to help readers understand what is available, distinguish this website from official DeepSeek services, and find reliable primary sources before making technical, financial, privacy, or business decisions.

Independence notice: Chat-Deep.ai is not DeepSeek’s official website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by DeepSeek. We do not represent DeepSeek and cannot access official DeepSeek accounts, API balances, billing records, internal systems, or support cases. DeepSeek and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.


### Our Mission

Our mission is to make DeepSeek-related information easier to understand and use without presenting independent commentary as official documentation. We aim to give readers a clear path from a question to the appropriate answer, tool, limitation, or official source.

- Explain DeepSeek models, API concepts, pricing structures, and common workflows in practical language.

- Provide independent browser-based chat access with clear information about how prompts are processed.

- Direct readers to official DeepSeek resources for accounts, API keys, billing, downloads, service status, policies, and support.

- Identify uncertainty, product limitations, safety considerations, and information that can change over time.

- Correct material errors when they are identified and make meaningful review dates visible where appropriate.


### What Chat-Deep.ai Provides

Chat-Deep.ai combines a technical utility with an independent publication. The website currently provides:

- Browser chat: an independent interface that uses the DeepSeek API and does not require a Chat-Deep.ai account.

- API and developer guides: explanations, examples, migration notes, security considerations, and links to official documentation.

- Pricing and model references: educational summaries of token pricing, model identifiers, capabilities, limits, and trade-offs, with reminders to verify time-sensitive details officially.

- Use-case and comparison content: practical guidance that separates documented facts from editorial analysis, examples, estimates, and observations.

- Access, privacy, safety, and troubleshooting guides: information intended to help users understand risks, avoid common mistakes, and reach the correct official destination when needed.

Our content is educational and informational. It does not replace official DeepSeek documentation, professional advice, or testing in your own environment.


### How the Chat Works

The browser chat on Chat-Deep.ai currently uses the DeepSeek API. When you submit a message, the prompt and the relevant context from your active conversation are sent through Chat-Deep.ai’s server-side infrastructure to the DeepSeek API so that a response can be generated and returned to your browser.

You do not need to create a Chat-Deep.ai account. Chat-Deep.ai does not provide permanent, account-based server history for browser-chat conversations. Local conversation history is enabled by default and may store up to 50 recent conversations in your browser on your device. Privacy Mode prevents new conversations from being added to that local history, but it does not stop chat processing.

This does not mean that using the chat involves zero processing or zero retention. Limited technical, security, and operational records may be processed or retained for a reasonable operational period by Chat-Deep.ai and its infrastructure providers. DeepSeek may also process, cache, or retain API data according to its applicable platform terms, policies, and technical operation. Do not enter passwords, API keys, payment information, confidential business material, medical records, or other sensitive personal data unless you have assessed the risks and have appropriate authorization and safeguards.

For details about data handling and protective measures, read our Privacy Policy and Security page. Direct use of DeepSeek’s own website, app, platform, or API account is governed by DeepSeek’s separate policies and terms.


### How We Research and Review Content

We use a source-led editorial process. For claims about official products, model names, API behavior, pricing, rate limits, policies, releases, and service availability, we prioritize relevant first-party DeepSeek sources. These may include DeepSeek’s official website, API documentation, platform pages, pricing pages, policy documents, status page, repositories, model documentation, and research publications.

When official sources do not answer a question completely, we may use reputable technical documentation, research papers, vendor documentation, or other clearly identified sources for context. Community reports can help identify possible issues, but they are not treated as primary proof of an official fact.

During editorial review, we aim to:

- Separate verified facts from interpretation, estimates, examples, and recommendations.

- Check time-sensitive claims against the most relevant official source available at the time of review.

- Avoid unsupported claims about official status, partnerships, pricing, free access, performance, privacy, security, or compliance.

- Explain important limitations and direct readers to official documentation for production or account-specific decisions.

- Update a page when a meaningful factual or practical change affects its usefulness.

We may use AI-assisted tools for tasks such as outlining, language improvement, formatting, or identifying possible gaps. AI assistance does not transfer editorial responsibility to the tool and must not be used to invent facts, citations, testing, endorsements, or official statements.

We do not claim that every article has been manually benchmarked or technically tested. When a page relies on direct testing, an independent check, or a calculation, we aim to identify the method, scope, date, assumptions, and limitations where those details are material.

Our full standards are described in the Editorial Policy.


### Updates and Corrections

DeepSeek models, API names, prices, limits, documentation, availability, and policies can change. A review date shows when a page received a meaningful editorial check; it is not a promise that every external service or detail will remain unchanged after that date.

If you find an inaccurate, outdated, unclear, or misleading statement, email info@chat-deep.ai or use our Contact page. Please include the affected URL, the exact statement, and a supporting source where possible. We review correction requests and update material errors when the available evidence supports a change.

- Our pricing examples and cost calculations are educational estimates, not quotes or billing guarantees.

- Our content is not legal, medical, financial, compliance, cybersecurity, or other professional advice.
